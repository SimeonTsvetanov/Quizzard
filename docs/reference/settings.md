# Settings

This page lists the home screen, the header, every entry of the menu and every option in **Settings**. To open the menu, select the button with three lines at the right end of the header. To open **Settings**, go to **Menu** > **Settings**.

## Home screen

The home screen has one card for each tool. A card shows the name of the tool, one line about it and **Open**. The tool you opened last is first. The **Quiz creator** card is planned and can't be opened.

| Card | What it does | Address |
|---|---|---|
| **Teams** | Splits names into 2 to 10 fair random teams. | `#/teams` |
| **Scores** | Keeps score for up to 50 players, with a leaderboard. | `#/scores` |
| **Answer sheet** | Records your team's answers and points round by round. | `#/sheet` |

## Header

Inside a tool, the header has the following buttons from left to right. Each has a name that screen readers read out.

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Back to Home** (arrow) | Goes back to the home screen. |
| The tool's own buttons | **Scores**: **Open leaderboard**, **Add player** and **Clear session**. **Answer sheet**: **Clear game**. **Teams** has none. |
| **Share app** | Shares the name and address of Quizzard. Shown only when the window is wider than 600 pixels; on a phone it is in the menu. |
| **Theme** | Goes through **System**, **Light** and **Dark**. Shown only when the window is wider than 600 pixels. |
| **Open menu** (three lines) | Opens the menu. |

## Settings

The following options are in **Settings**, in the order of the window.

| Option | What it does | Values | Default |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Theme** | Sets the colours of Quizzard. | **System**, **Light**, **Dark** | **System** (follows your device) |
| **Background** | Behind every screen: a starry night sky in the dark theme and soft mountains under the sun in the light theme, moving slowly; **Simple** is a quiet glow. Nothing moves when your device asks for reduced motion. | **Animated**, **Simple** | **Animated** |
| **Language** | Sets the language of Quizzard, its tools and **Help**. | **English**, **Български** | **English** |
| **Your data** | Lists the data each tool and Quizzard itself saved, with its size. **Clear** deletes the data of one tool, after you confirm. Shows **Nothing saved** for an empty tool. | **Teams**, **Scores**, **Answer sheet**, **Quizzard settings** | — |
| **Space used** | The space the browser reports for this website, including the offline copy. It is not the size of your data. | — | — |
| **Kept permanently** | Whether the browser promises not to clear Quizzard's data when space runs low. | **Yes ✓**, **Not yet** | **Not yet** |
| **Keep data on this device** | Asks the browser to keep the data permanently. | — | — |
| **Export my data** | Saves the settings and the data of every tool in one file. | `quizzard-data-YYYY-MM-DD.json` | — |
| **Import data** | Replaces the data on this device with the content of an export file, after you confirm. Files from other apps are refused. | An export file of up to 50 MB | — |
| **Clear my data** | Deletes everything Quizzard and its tools saved on this device, after you confirm. Other apps aren't affected. | — | — |

**Quizzard settings** in **Your data** has no **Clear**. **Clear my data** deletes it with everything else.

## Menu

The menu has four groups. The following table lists the entries in the order of the menu.

| Group | Entry | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Use | **Help** | Opens the help. Inside a tool, the section of that tool comes first. Works offline. |
| Use | **Settings** | Opens [Settings](#settings). |
| Use | **What's new** | Lists the changes of recent versions. |
| App | **Share app** | Shares the name and address of Quizzard with the share sheet, or opens a window with **WhatsApp** and **Copy**. |
| App | **Install app** | Installs Quizzard, or shows the steps. Reads **Installed ✓** when installed. |
| App | **Report a problem** | Opens a form. See [Report a problem](#report-a-problem). |
| About | **About** | Shows the version, the author, the open-source projects Quizzard uses and their licence texts. |
| About | **Terms**, **Privacy**, **AI notice**, **Legal notice**, **Contact** | Open the policy and contact pages inside the app. |
| Support | **Support ☕**, **GitHub**, **LinkedIn** | Open the author's support page, the source code and the author's profile. |

### Report a problem

The **Report a problem** form has a **Type** (**Problem**, **Idea** or **Question**), a **Short title** and a **What happened / your idea** field. A list of technical details is attached, and you can untick each one: the app version, your browser, the screen size, whether Quizzard is installed, your browser's language and the time. They never contain names, players, scores or answers. Quizzard sends nothing by itself. **Open on GitHub** opens GitHub with your text filled in. The issue is public and needs a free GitHub account.
